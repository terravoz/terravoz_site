About

Terravoz is a strategy, research and technology development company that works with government, non-profit and grassroots organizations in the implementation of voice, SMS and web-based solutions for open governance, social inclusion and community empowerment.

Our mission is to build participatory communication systems that reach out to every single person in a given region, independently of the level of technology access that people might have.

Applications of our technology include:

  • "Get Out to Vote” campaigns
  • 2-1-1 and 3-1-1 community hotlines
  • City-wide information systems
  • Phone-, web- and SMS-based surveys
  • Group communication
  • Story recording / playback
  • Community voicemail
  • Audio neighborhood tours
  • Place-based adventure games
  • Interactive community radio programs
  • Emergency announcements

Stay tuned! Terravoz will redefine the way you interact with your phone, the web, and the people around you!



Our mission is to meet the information needs of the most underserved individuals — 10 to 17 million in the U.S. and 5.6 billion worldwide — who rely on basic mobile phones as their main form of technological access.

Services

Terravoz's products are built on our extensive collection of simple, ready-made open source modules that bring the power of voice, touch-phone telephony, and SMS to connect technologically-challenged communities to the Internet.

Key benefits of our unique, non-proprietary products include:

• Easy installation and configuration.
• No additional programming needed.
• Easy to customize. Only use the features you need.
• The ability to unify web, email, voice, and SMS.
• Sustainably develop scalable projects
• Users don't have to have a costly data plan
• Support from a community of open-source developers
• For developers: our VoIP Drupal platform is built on a visual programming language for fast prototyping

Portfolio

Our clients include governments, non-profits, and grassroots organizations such as The World Bank, MIT's Center for Civic Media, Studio REV-, and The National Domestic Workers Alliance. Our products have been showcased at The White House, on BBC, GOOD magazine, New York Times, conferences and universities worldwide.

OnTrack

a World Bank system designed to foster civic participation via phone, web and SMS for communities in Bolivia, Nepal and Zambia. View item

VoJo
